About

I am a Ph.D. student at Université de Rennes, working on medical image analysis.

Previously, I received my M.S. from Southeast University (东南大学, 2022–2025), where I was advised by Prof. Huazhong Shu and Jiasong Wu. My research sits at the intersection of 3D shape priors, identity modelling and clinical imaging.
